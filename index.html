How To Spot Repairable Damage In 30 Seconds

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make is assuming all damage is the same.

Some damage is cosmetic.

Some damage is structural.

Learning the difference can save you a lot of money and help you identify profitable opportunities.

When evaluating leather furniture, I look at:

  • Tears and cuts
  • Surface wear
  • Color loss
  • Cushion condition
  • Overall structure

Many pieces that appear heavily damaged are actually excellent candidates for repair.
